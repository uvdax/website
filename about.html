About Us

Three decades in, UV Dax is still run the way it started: hands-on, in-house, and answerable only to the work. What began with one man and a Roland plotter in 1999 is now Jammu’s largest full-pipeline signage and large-format print operation — built, deliberately, one demanding client at a time.

Balbir Singh

Founder & Chief Visionary

"Precision isn't an option; it's the baseline of everything we build."

1999

The Foundation

Started operations with a single Roland plotter, focusin' on high-precision vinyl cutting and personal craftsmanship.

2010

Infrastructure Expansion

Scaled operations to a 5,000 sq.ft workshop to handle full-scale architectural signage and heavy metal fabrication.

2020

HP Latex & Eco Transition

Pioneered eco-friendly water-based printing technology with a dedicated 500 sq.ft print lab setup.

Present Day

Full-Pipeline Leadership

Dominatin' the regional signage industry with end-to-end design, pre-press, fabrication, and installation capability.

01 / DIRECTION

Vision

To make UV Dax synonymous with signage that's engineered, not just printed — trusted at any scale, anywhere it's installed.

02 / PURPOSE

Mission

We design, print, fabricate, and install signage and large-format branding entirely in-house — pre-press through final installation — for brands that can't afford a partner who cuts corners. What started with one Roland plotter in 1999 is now a production floor built for volume most signage shops in the region simply can't take on.
